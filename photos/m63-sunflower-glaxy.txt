Title: M63 Sunflower Glaxy
Date: 2023-03-01 20:02
Category: Galaxy

This is M63, the Sunflower Galaxy, which lies some 29.3 million lightyears away from us. The data was taken with 104 pictures taken over 13 hours using the telescope.live 70cm Telescope in Spain. Processed by me in Photoshop, Pixinsight, and some nice finishes in Topaz Adjust AI.
