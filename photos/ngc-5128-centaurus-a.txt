Title: NGC 5128 Centaurus A
Date: 2023-03-19 16:37
Category: Galaxy

Centaurus A (NGC 5128) is a galaxy in the constellation of Centaurus.  It is the fifth brightest galaxy in the sky and its supermassive black hole ejects relativistic jets in X-Ray and Radio wavelengths.  Taken with Telescope.Live data from the CHI-1 Telescope in Chile, a Planewave CDK 24” scope
